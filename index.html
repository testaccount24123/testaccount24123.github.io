👋 What This Software Does

Telegram is a great place for long conversations, but the chat history lives on Telegram's servers, not on your computer. If a group is deleted, renamed, or you simply lose access to it, years of discussion can disappear overnight. tg-chat-dump solves that problem by copying an entire chat or forum down to your own machine.

You point it at a chat, press start, and it works through the whole history for you. When it finishes, you have a tidy set of folders you can browse, search, and back up like any other document on your PC. It is built for people who care about keeping their own records — researchers, community managers, moderators, journalists, and anyone who has ever thought "I wish I had saved that."

⚡ Tips for a Smooth First Run

  • Start with a small chat the first time, just to see what the finished folders look like. Once you are comfortable, move on to the big one.
  • Keep your computer plugged in and awake while a large backup runs. Sleep mode pauses the work, though nothing is lost.
  • Use a wired or stable internet connection for very large chats. The faster your connection, the faster the copy completes.
  • Decide up front whether you want media files included. Text-only backups are much smaller and finish far quicker.
  • Run it on a drive with plenty of free space. Chats with lots of photos and videos can take up a surprising amount of room.

🔁 Stopping and Resuming

Large chats can take a while, and you are not expected to sit and watch. Close the window, shut down your computer, or lose your connection — it genuinely does not matter. The next time you start a run on the same chat and the same folder, the software reads its progress file and carries on from the first message it has not yet saved.

This also means you can split a job across several days without thinking about it. Nothing gets duplicated, and nothing gets skipped.

🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Everything you copy stays on your own computer. There is no cloud account, no upload step, and no third-party service in the middle. Your chat history is written straight to the folder you selected.

Only save chats you have the right to save. If a group has rules about exporting content, or if you are handling other people's private messages, check with the people involved and follow any local laws that apply to personal data where you live. A backup is a tool — how you use it is up to you.
